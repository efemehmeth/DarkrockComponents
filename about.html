// The Story

One builder.
Zero shortcuts.

I'm Efe. 21 years old. I run a CNC lathe and mill in my own workshop and I make mountain bike parts that I genuinely wish existed when I was building my first bike.

// Background

How it started.

It started with frustration. I kept looking at MTB components on the market and thinking — these tolerances are sloppy, this material is mediocre, and I'm paying premium prices for something I could make better myself.

So I did. I got access to a 5-axis CNC machining centre, taught myself CAM programming, and started designing parts from the ground up — every dimension calculated, every surface finish intentional.

DarkRock isn't a factory. It's a workshop. Every order is taken seriously because every part has my name on it.

// How It's Made

From billet to bike.

01

Design & Engineering

Every part starts in CAD. Geometry is obsessed over — wall thicknesses, stress concentrations, interface dimensions. Nothing ships until the model is right.

02

Material Selection

7075-T6 aluminium billet, sourced from certified suppliers. The same alloy used in aerospace applications — not the cheap 6061 most brands default to.

03

5-Axis CNC Machining

Machined in multiple setups on a 5-axis centre. Tight tolerances held throughout. Every cut is programmed and verified before touching the billet.

04

Finishing & Inspection

Deburred by hand, anodized, and individually inspected against drawing before packing. No batch releases. Every part checked one by one.

// Materials & Standards

By the numbers.

🔬

7075-T6 Aluminium

Tensile strength up to 572 MPa. Over 40% stronger than 6061 while maintaining similar weight.

📏

±0.01mm Tolerance

Critical interfaces machined to within one-hundredth of a millimetre. Consistent across every part.

🛡️

Type III Anodize

Hard-anodized to 25–30μm depth for maximum surface hardness and corrosion resistance.

✅

100% Inspected

Every single component measured before shipping. No exceptions, no sampling, no compromise.
